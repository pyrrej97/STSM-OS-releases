# STSM OS downloads

STSM OS helps the South Texas School of Ministry make its spreadsheets, documents and slides in the STSM style,
by asking for them in plain English.

## Download

**[Download STSM OS 0.2.0 (ZIP)](https://github.com/pyrrej97/STSM-OS-releases/releases/latest/download/STSM-OS-0.2.0.zip)**

All versions: [Releases](https://github.com/pyrrej97/STSM-OS-releases/releases)

## Install

**Windows 10 or 11:** right-click the ZIP in Downloads, choose **Extract All**, choose **Documents**, open the new
STSM-OS folder and double-click **Setup STSM OS**. If Windows says it protected your PC, click **More info**, then
**Run anyway**.

**Mac (macOS 14 or newer):** Safari unpacks the ZIP into Downloads (or double-click it). Drag the STSM-OS folder into
**Documents**, open it and double-click **Setup STSM OS**. If macOS says it can't check it, click **Done**, open
**System Settings > Privacy & Security**, scroll down, click **Open Anyway**, then double-click Setup again.

Setup asks one question, then installs everything (about 3.5 GB, 10 to 30 minutes). When it finishes, the Welcome
page opens and OpenCode is ready for your first request. The **STSM OS Setup Guide** in the folder shows every step.

The STSM logo belongs to the South Texas School of Ministry (New Life Church).
